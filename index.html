Banter

Live sports chat, with the other side in the room.

Banter is a social app built around what is happening right now in football and motorsport. Pick the match or race, post, react and trade banter with supporters of both sides before, during and after the event. Banter is for people aged 18 and over.

Need help?

Email us at kirk@you-nameit.co.za. For account deletion you can use Profile → Delete account in the app.
