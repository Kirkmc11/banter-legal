Three taps to share your take

No chat rooms to hunt through and no followers to build. Every game has its own feed, made for opinions.

  1. Pick your game

    Open a live match or race, or one coming up. Your team's games come first.

  2. Drop your take

    A hot take, a wind-up or a bold prediction. Your supporter badge shows whose colours you wear.

  3. Take on the rivals

    The other side will answer. React, reply and go toe to toe. Teasing is welcome. Abuse is not.

Popular, or just what is happening now

Every game has two tabs. Popular lifts the posts people are reacting to, and rewards the ones both sets of fans agree on. Recent is the live stream, newest first.

  • Home, Away and All filters on every match
  • Applause, like, boo and banter reactions that show which side they came from
  • Pre-match, live and post-match, all in one place
  • Fixtures, live scores and league tables for the games you care about

Play hard but play fair!

Wind-ups and rivalry are part of the game. Targeting a person is not. Banter 11 is for people aged 18 and over, and it is built to keep the contest fair.

  • Report any post or photo, and block anyone you do not want to hear from
  • Posts with enough reports are hidden while a moderator reviews them
  • No racism, threats, harassment or content you do not have the right to share
  • Copyright complaints are taken seriously. Read how to send one
