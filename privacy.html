Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Banter app collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. Banter is operated by Kirk McMahon, South Africa ("we", "us"). Contact: kirk@you-nameit.co.za. We act as the responsible party (POPIA) and the controller (UK GDPR) for the information described here.

1. Who Banter is for

Banter is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and block anyone under 18. If we learn that an under-18 has an account, we will delete it.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Sign-in details: your email address and a sign-in identifier from Apple, Google or email sign-in. We do not see your Apple or Google password.To create and secure your account.
Profile: display name, optional profile picture, date of birth (used only to check you are 18+), favourite team in each competition, teams you follow.To show your supporter badge and personalise your feed.

Moderation records: reports about you or by you, moderator actions, suspensions.To enforce our Terms and protect other users.
Ban record: if you are permanently banned, a one-way hash (a scrambled code, not your email) of your sign-in identity.To stop banned people signing straight back up.

Usage data (Firebase Analytics): which screens you open and which features you use, such as opening a match or adding a reaction. It never includes the text of your posts.To understand how the app is used and improve it.
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): error details, app version, device model and operating system version.To find and fix bugs.
Advertising data (Google AdMob): device and advertising identifiers and ad interaction, used according to your consent choice.To show ads, which pay for the app.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or financial information.

3. Our legal basis (UK and EU users)

  • Contract: running your account, posts and notifications.
  • Legitimate interests: security, moderation, preventing ban evasion, fixing crashes.
  • Consent: personalised ads and, where the law requires it, usage analytics and crash reports. You can change your choice at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or hand over information by law.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers to run Banter:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google (Firebase and AdMob): analytics, crash reports, push notifications and advertising.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, and push delivery on iPhones.
  • Sports data providers: we receive match and race data from them. We do not send them your personal information.

Your display name, profile picture, supporter badge, posts and reactions are visible to other Banter users. Please do not post personal information you want to keep private. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect people from serious harm.

5. Other countries

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the provider's certified transfer frameworks.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Ban record: up to 2 years.
  • Analytics and crash data: held by Google for a limited period under our settings, and not linked to your name.
  • Moderation records and content under review: as long as needed to finish the review, and as long as we need to meet legal obligations or protect other users.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Profile, or by emailing kirk@you-nameit.co.za. When you delete:

  • Your profile, sign-in record, favourites, follows, blocks, predictions, reactions and off-topic flags are removed.
  • Your posts are deleted. A post that other people have replied to stays in the thread as a blank "deleted" post that is no longer linked to you.
  • If a post is under active report review when you delete, we keep a copy for moderators until the review ends, and it is not linked to your name once your account is gone.
  • If you were permanently banned, we keep the one-way ban record for up to 2 years.
  • Usage data already sent to Google Analytics is reset for your device. Backups are overwritten on a normal schedule.

When you delete one of your own posts, its text is removed from the app. We keep a copy privately so moderators can still act on abuse.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Usage data and crash reports: turn off Share usage data in Profile at any time.
  • Ads: change your ad consent from the privacy options in the app. You can also reset your device's advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Notifications: change them in the app or your phone settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability: you can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop certain uses of it. Email kirk@you-nameit.co.za. We reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm it is you first.

If you are not happy with our response you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za, or, if you are in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would like the chance to fix it first.

9. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and database rules so people can only see what they should. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before it takes effect.
